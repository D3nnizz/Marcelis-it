Privacy Policy

RijsTijd  ·  Last updated: May 7, 2026

RijsTijd is an offline bread-baking timer app. We do not collect, store, or share any personal data.

Data we do NOT collect

  • No accounts or login
  • No names, email addresses, or contact information
  • No location data
  • No analytics or usage tracking
  • No advertising identifiers
  • No data is ever sent to any server — the app works entirely on your device

Data stored locally on your device

  • Recipes you create (ingredients, steps, timers)
  • Photos you choose to attach to recipes (stored in the app's private folder)
  • Your active timer sessions
  • Your subscription status

This data never leaves your device and is deleted when you uninstall the app.

Permissions used

  • Camera / Photo Library — only to let you add photos to your own recipes. No images are uploaded.
  • Notifications — only to alert you when a timer finishes (local, not push notifications).

In-app purchases

Payment is processed by Apple App Store or Google Play. We do not see or store any payment or billing information.
